Course Curriculum

What will you learn?

A structured path from marketing basics to AI-supported campaigns, analytics and automation.

01

Digital Marketing Fundamentals

Understand marketing, branding, sales funnels, consumer behaviour and the digital channel mix.

02

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

On-page, off-page and technical SEO, keyword research, audits and search performance tools.

03

Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

PPC fundamentals, keyword targeting, bidding, ad rank, budget planning and campaign reporting.

04

Google Ads

Search and shopping campaign setup, Ad copies, extensions, targeting and optimisation reports.

05

Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram campaign structures, audience creation, lead ads and creative testing.

06

Social Media Marketing

Channel strategy, content calendars, organic posting, community handling and social advertising.

07

Content Marketing

Content research, brand storytelling, calendars, copywriting and content for each funnel stage.

08

Email Marketing

List building, campaign structure, subject lines, segmentation and basic performance tracking.

09

Marketing Analytics

GA4, Search Console and dashboard reporting to understand traffic, leads and campaign results.

10

AI Tools for Digital Marketing

Use AI for keyword research, content drafts, creative ideas, reporting and answer-engine visibility.

11

Marketing Automation

Automate repetitive marketing tasks, lead handling and workflow reporting with suitable tools.

Career Opportunities

Roles you can prepare for

These are common digital marketing career paths. Completion of training does not guarantee a job.

Digital Marketing Executive

Coordinate campaigns across channels, track results and support daily marketing activity.

SEO Specialist

Improve search visibility through technical fixes, content planning and performance audits.

Performance Marketer

Plan and optimise paid campaigns with a focus on leads, conversions and return on ad spend.

Social Media Manager

Build content calendars, manage accounts, engage audiences and report on social performance.

PPC Specialist

Manage search and display advertising, keyword bidding, ad copy and campaign reporting.

Digital Marketing Consultant

Advise businesses on channel strategy, funnels, content and measurement after gaining experience.
